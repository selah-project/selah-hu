## The case-suffix ruling, at scale

Hungarian suffixation lengthens the final vowel: Jahve → Jahvét
(accusative), Jahvénak (dative), Jahvéhoz, Jahvétól, Jahvéban…
Per the pre-burn ruling (grammar, not erasure): **1,092 verses
carry suffixed Jahvé; zero title-welds found** (no "az Úr Jahve"
fusions; stem intact everywhere). The sealing census counts the
full stem (`\bjahv[eé]\w*`), which is why the seal is
sibling-comparable. Ratification of the ruling rides the shared
ro+hu+kk case-suffix question, PENDING SCOTT.
